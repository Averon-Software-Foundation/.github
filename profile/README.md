# Averon Software Foundation

**Building software with purpose.**

Averon Software Foundation is an independent software organisation focused on developing, maintaining and supporting software projects.

We believe software should be thoughtfully designed, accessible and built to last. Our goal is to create useful tools, explore new ideas and contribute to the wider open-source ecosystem.

---

## What We Do

- **Software Development** — Designing and building reliable, well-engineered software.
- **Open Source** — Supporting open development, collaboration and community-driven projects.
- **Community** — Encouraging collaboration and making software accessible to everyone.

## Our Projects

Explore our repositories to discover the software and tools being developed under Averon.

All projects are maintained within this organisation, with individual repositories containing their own documentation, licensing and contribution guidelines.

## Get Involved

We welcome developers, contributors and anyone interested in building meaningful software.

Whether you're reporting an issue, suggesting an improvement or contributing code, your involvement is appreciated.

Please refer to the contribution guidelines within individual repositories before submitting changes.
